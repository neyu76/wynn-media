VIDEO 09
TITLE: The Burglar Who Only Moved Furniture
NHÓM: MƯU MẸO, BẪY VÀ LỖ HỔNG
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

A man snuck into a family's house every night and moved every single thing one inch to the left. The first night, nobody notices, because who measures their couch? But by the end of the week, the fridge is blocking the back door, and the dad is pretty sure he's losing his mind.

So, they call the police. The officer looks at the couch, then at the dad, and goes, “So someone broke in to move your furniture an inch?” He laughs all the way back to his car. They start locking every door and window at night, but every morning, everything has still crept a little further, until the bed is pressed up against the wall.

At this point, their daughter swears she hears something sliding across the floor at 3am, so the dad camps out on the couch with a baseball bat, ready to catch whoever's doing this. He wakes up the next morning, and the couch is one inch to the left, with him still on it. By the end of the month, the couch is halfway across the living room, and the family can't take it anymore.

They sell the house for half what they paid, and the buyer is him. You see, he'd sold them that house a year earlier and kept one key. So, all it took was a month of sliding furniture to buy it back at half the price.
