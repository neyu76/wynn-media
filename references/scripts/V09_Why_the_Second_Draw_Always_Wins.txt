VIDEO 11
TITLE: Why the Second Draw Always Wins
NHÓM: MƯU MẸO, BẪY VÀ LỖ HỔNG
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

The fastest gun in the West had one rule: never draw first. Imagine you're standing in the street at noon, staring down a guy everyone calls Slow Eddie. His hands are at his sides, he hasn't even looked at his gun, and he just nods at you and says, “After you.” So you grab for your gun faster than you've ever moved in your life, yet you're face down in the dirt before it clears the holster.

How is that possible? Eddie isn't even fast. He'd lose a race to the saloon against a guy carrying two beers.

But men ride in from three states away to duel him, and every one of them goes down the exact same way. The only weird thing anyone ever notices is that Eddie never looks at your face. He just stares at your hand.

You see, think about the last time your phone slid off a table. You caught it before you even realized it was falling. Because you never decided to catch it.

Your body just did. But when you went for your gun, you had to decide the exact moment to draw. And that tiny decision slowed your hand down.

Eddie never decides anything. He just waits for your hand to twitch and lets his body react on its own.
