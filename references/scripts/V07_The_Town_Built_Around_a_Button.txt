VIDEO 16
TITLE: The Town Built Around a Button
NHÓM: TRUYỆN KỂ, NGHỊCH LÝ VÀ BÍ ẨN
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

There's a button in the middle of town, and nobody's allowed to press it. The whole town is built around it in perfect circles, every house facing the center, where the button sits under a glass case, with two guards who never take their eyes off it. Around all of it is a wall so tall nobody's ever seen over it.

At night, a glow comes from the other side, and every year it gets a little dimmer. But Gary's always noticed two weird things. Under the glass, there are 47 tiny scratches nobody can explain, and the statue of the town's founders has a woman who looks exactly like his mom.

So when his friends bet him 5 bucks he won't press it, he can't say no. At 3 in the morning, the guard swaps shifts, and he's got about 30 seconds. He lifts the glass, holds his breath, and presses it, but nothing happens.

Just a tiny scratching sound under the glass. But as he walks home, the glow over the wall is brighter than it's ever been. The next morning, Gary wakes up in the grass.

The houses are gone, the guards are gone, and the whole town is standing in an empty field around the button, staring at it like they've never seen it before. Around the button, there are 48 scratches. Then his mom steps out of the crowd and says, “Nobody touches this.

We'll build our town right here.”
