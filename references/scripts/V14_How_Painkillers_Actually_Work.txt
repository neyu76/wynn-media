TITLE: How Painkillers Actually Work
NHÓM: KHOA HỌC VÀ NÃO BỘ
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

How do painkillers know where the pain is? You swallow one for a headache, and then what? Does it pull out a little map?

All you know is it melts in your stomach, slips into your blood, and 20 minutes later, your headache's gone. So how does a pill with no brain find the one spot that hurts? Well, picture your body as a house with thousands of rooms and a smoke alarm in every single one.

When you get hurt, the alarm in that room starts screaming, and that's the pain you feel. Now, here comes the painkiller. It kicks the front door in and rips the batteries out of every single alarm in the house.

Every other alarm was already silent, so the only one you notice go quiet is the one in your head.
