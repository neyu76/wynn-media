VIDEO 12
TITLE: An Unstoppable Spear vs an Unbreakable Shield
NHÓM: TRUYỆN KỂ, NGHỊCH LÝ VÀ BÍ ẨN
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

A blacksmith sold a spear that could pierce anything to one king and a shield that nothing could pierce to his worst enemy. The two kingdoms had been at war for a hundred years, and now each king finally had the weapon to end it. So both armies marched to the same field, the spear at the front of one and the shield at the front of the other.

Then nobody moved. A whole week went by, with 10,000 soldiers just staring at two pieces of metal. One night, each king secretly sent a messenger to the blacksmith with the same question.

“Which one is real?” And to both of them, he gave the same answer: “Attack and find out.” You see, a spear that pierces anything and a shield nothing can pierce can't both exist. So the blacksmith made both out of plain old iron, knowing neither king would dare be the first to find out. And that's how he ended the war.
