VIDEO 06
TITLE: How a Mirror Rigged a Horse Race
NHÓM: MƯU MẸO, BẪY VÀ LỖ HỔNG
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

He bet his life savings on the slowest horse in the race. The whole betting room is laughing at him because all day long he hasn't bet a single dollar. He just sits by the window, smiling, because he has a mirror.

Wait, what's a mirror going to do? Well, the racetrack is right over the hill from town, and his buddy is up on that hill with the biggest mirror he could find.

Race after race, the favorite wins, and the hill stays dark. And by the fifth, the regulars are betting on whether he'll ever bet. “Hey, mirror man. Maybe try wiping it.”

In the last race, the favorite's so far ahead its jockey is waving to his mom, and horse nine is so far behind it's practically in the parking lot. Then the hill flashes, and he puts every dollar he has on horse nine.

The guy behind the counter almost feels bad taking it. “Pal, that horse lost a race to the milk cart, and it was pulling the milk cart.”

You see, back then, betting rooms didn't find out who won until the winner came clicking in over a wire. And until it did, bets stayed open.

Up on that hill, his buddy just watched horse nine come out of nowhere and steal it by a nose. So while the room is still laughing, the wire starts clicking, and by the time it spells out nine, nobody's laughing anymore.
