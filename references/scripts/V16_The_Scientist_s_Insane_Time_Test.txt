VIDEO 04
TITLE: The Scientist's Insane Time Test
NHÓM: KHOA HỌC VÀ NÃO BỘ
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

A scientist dropped people off a tower just to find out what happens to time right before you die. Apparently, when you're terrified, your brain speeds up so much that the whole world slows down. People who survive crashes swear everything went into slow motion.

So before they fell, he strapped a little screen to each of their wrists that flashed numbers way too fast to read. If time really slowed down, they'd be able to read them as they were falling. When they landed, every one of them swore the fall took way longer than it really did.

But when he asked them what number they saw, not one of them could tell him. You see, when you're frightened, your brain records way more detail than normal. So when you replay it later, it feels like it all happened in slow motion.
