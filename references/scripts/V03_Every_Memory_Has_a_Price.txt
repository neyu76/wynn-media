VIDEO 15
TITLE: Every Memory Has a Price
NHÓM: TRUYỆN KỂ, NGHỊCH LÝ VÀ BÍ ẨN
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

He sold his happiest memory to a stranger behind a counter. You see, memories are money here. There's a shop on every corner that'll pull one right out of your skull.

Landlords take rent in birthdays, and a bag of groceries costs the day you learn to ride a bike. The city is split in two by a giant wall. On one side, the rich just buy everyone else's summers.

So some guy over there has been to the Bahamas 300 times without leaving his couch. On the other side, people stare at old photos of themselves smiling, with no idea why. And since the rich only want the good stuff, the happier the memory, the more it pays.

Jerry only sold his to cover rent. But that same week, his little brother gets sick. And every night in the hospital, he begs for the same bedtime story.

The summer they caught a frog the size of a football at the lake. But the medicine costs way more than rent. So Jerry goes back and sells that summer for a week of it.

The best birthday he ever had pays for the surgery. And soon, he's selling whole years until his head is mostly bad days and bus rides. His brother keeps getting worse until there's only one good year left.

And the guy behind the counter stares at him and says, “Kid, you sell this, there's nothing happy left in there.” He sells it anyway, and it pays for everything. A week later, his brother sits up in bed, cured, and says, “Hey, Jerry, tell me the frog story.” And Jerry has no idea who this kid is. He sold every happy year he ever had, and his brother was in every single one.
