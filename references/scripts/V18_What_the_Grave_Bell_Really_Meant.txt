VIDEO 14
TITLE: What the Grave Bell Really Meant
NHÓM: TRUYỆN KỂ, NGHỊCH LÝ VÀ BÍ ẨN
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

They buried a man alive with a bell tied to his finger. You see, people were so scared of waking up in a coffin that graves came with a little bell on top, tied by a string to the body's finger. Except when his bell starts ringing at 2 in the morning, the guy next door decides it's probably the wind.

Then it rings faster, and now the whole street is out on their porches, going, “You go check.” “No, you go check.” Somebody drags the doctor out of bed, and he goes, “Bodies twitch. He's dead. I signed the paper myself.” But his wife isn't on her porch.

She's sitting in the dark with a shovel by the door because she knows that ring: three short, one long. The exact signal they practiced. Wait, practiced?

Well, he was never dead. He owed money to half the town, so he faked his death. And the second that bell rang, she was supposed to grab the shovel, dig him up, and skip town with him.

Instead, she just sits there all night, listening to it ring, fast and slow, then once every few minutes, until sunrise, when it finally stops. You see, with him in the ground, every debt got buried right along with him, and the house was all hers.
