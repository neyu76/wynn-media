VIDEO 07
TITLE: He Could Spot Winning Tickets Just by Looking
NHÓM: MƯU MẸO, BẪY VÀ LỖ HỔNG
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

Look at these two scratch cards. One's a winner and one's garbage. Yet there's a way to tell without scratching either of them.

It all started when a guy got fired and spent five bucks on two scratch cards on the way home. He scratches one and wins his five bucks back, but instead of celebrating like a normal person, he gets suspicious. If the lottery decides exactly how many winners get printed, how does the machine know which ones they are?

So, he does what any sane person would do. He blows his entire severance check on a huge stack, spreads them across his apartment floor, and stares until something jumps out. Every card has little grids of numbers printed right on the front, and most of the numbers show up two or three times, but a few only show up once.

And whenever three of those loners line up in a row, it's a winner! Which means this guy just cracked the lottery. He could walk into every gas station in the state and clean them out.

So what does he do? He calls the lottery. “Sir, that's impossible.” Okay, so he walks into their office with a box full of cards he's never scratched, sorted into winners and losers, and gets 90% of them right.

By the end of the week, the game is gone from every store.
