VIDEO 10
TITLE: This Knight Found the Ultimate Loophole
NHÓM: MƯU MẸO, BẪY VÀ LỖ HỔNG
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

A knight paid a priest to forgive a crime he hadn't committed yet. You see, the priest was traveling from town to town selling forgiveness for coins. Lying had a price, stealing had a price, and the worse the sin, the more you paid.

People lined up for hours to drop their money into his huge iron chest. So, the knight stepped up and asked to be forgiven for something he was planning to do next week. The priest hesitated, charged him extra, and stamped the paper anyway.

A few days later, the priest left town through the forest, with the chest so full, it took two men to carry it. That's where the knight was waiting. He took every last coin, and when the priest screamed that he'd go to hell for it, the knight just held up the paper.

The crime he'd paid to be forgiven for was robbing the man who sold it to him.
