VIDEO 01
TITLE: Would You Upload Your Brain?
NHÓM: KHOA HỌC VÀ NÃO BỘ
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

If you took your brain out and put it in a jar, you'd die. But your brain wouldn't. Without blood, it loses millions of neurons every minute.

But drop it in a jar, hook it up to a machine pumping in oxygen, and it could keep firing, maybe even thinking. Problem is, it would still get old. So, some scientists want to skip the jar.

Every memory you have is just a pattern of connections. Copy that pattern into a computer, and you'd technically be immortal. Well, unless someone trips over the power cable.

And it's already happening. This year, a company copied a fruit fly's entire brain into a computer, gave it a virtual body, and switched it on. It just started walking around looking for food.

It had no idea it wasn't real. The catch? To copy yours, they'd have to pump your brain full of chemicals while you're still alive, then slice it a thousand times thinner than a hair.

One company actually offered it. The procedure was 100% fatal. People signed up anyway.

Here's the creepy part. Even if it works, the thing that wakes up in the computer remembers your whole life. It swears it's you, but you're still lying on the table.

So, would you still sign up?
