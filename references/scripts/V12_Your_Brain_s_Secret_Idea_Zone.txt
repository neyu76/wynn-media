VIDEO 02
TITLE: Your Brain's Secret Idea Zone
NHÓM: KHOA HỌC VÀ NÃO BỘ
NGÔN NGỮ: Tiếng Anh (nguyên bản)

======================================================================
KỊCH BẢN / TRANSCRIPT
======================================================================

If you fall asleep holding two steel balls, you might wake up a genius. You see, first, you put two metal plates on the floor, sit down right above them, and hold a steel ball in each hand. Then, you just let yourself drift off.

Now, here's the weird part. The second you fall asleep, your hands go limp, the balls smash into the plates, and the noise jolts you awake. Sounds like the worst nap ever.

But Thomas Edison was rumored to do it on purpose. That's because right before deep sleep, your brain hits a strange halfway state called hypnagogia, where it starts connecting ideas it never would while you're awake. Normally, you sink right past it and forget everything, but the crash yanks you out right in the middle of it, so you wake up holding the idea instead of the balls.
