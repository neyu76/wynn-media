In this made-up town, the ticket machine fined its own tickets.
The mayor had one simple rule: leave paper on the street for three seconds, and you get a fine. If a machine drops it, the machine's owner pays.
To enforce the rule, he installed an automatic ticket printer. It spotted a scrap, waited three seconds, and printed a fine. The ticket fluttered straight onto the pavement.
Three seconds later, the machine spotted its own ticket. Another fine. Another piece of paper. Another countdown. The street cleaner hurried over, but every ticket he reached for had already earned a companion.
The mayor pointed at the mess, then at the cleaner. The cleaner pointed at the rule, then crouched beside the machine. There was the ownership plate: Town Hall.
He switched off the printer, gathered the tickets, and carried the entire pile into the mayor's office. Then he laid the ownership plate on top. The street was finally clean. The mayor's desk had disappeared.
Please fine the owner.
