One extra pour, and this cup starts emptying itself.
At first, it behaves perfectly. A little water goes in, and stays there. Our thirsty customer decides that a little more would be even better.
So he pours past the mark. Water suddenly runs out through the stem. He stops pouring. The cup keeps draining. A tray underneath is getting his drink instead.
You see, the middle of this cup hides a siphon. Its passage rises to a high point, then turns downward toward an outlet below the cup.
Below that high point, the water stays put. Fill it far enough to start the siphon, and water keeps flowing down the outlet, even after the level drops below the mark.
It stops when the water reaches the intake opening and air gets in. A little may remain at the bottom, depending on the design. Our customer wanted a fuller cup. Now he is looking at a fuller tray.
More water. Less drink.
